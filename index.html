Financial Statements Lab

Learn the statements. Connect the numbers. Understand the business.

Three statements describe almost every business: what it earned, what it owns and owes, and where its cash went. This lab lets you click through each line, see how the numbers connect, and test yourself, using one fictional company over three years.

Please note: all company data here is fictional and illustrative. It is not taken from any real company.
